## 5. Integrity notes

- 20,000 new ideas were generated by a deterministic combinatorial composer (seeded RNG)
  over hand-authored pattern/lens/context/architecture/harness corpora — not LLM-freeform;
  this is what makes 20k auditable and dedupable.
- Heuristic scores are priors, not judgments; they only gate the funnel. The ranked,
  novelty-checked top-64 bracket and the diverse council slate (in-context judging with the
  full updated rules) are the actual decision layers.
- Novelty check is lexical (title+concept tokens vs repo/model/space names+descriptions,
  J≥0.40). It verifies *no named prior work*, not *no conceptual cousin*; the council
  additionally assessed concept-level collisions (this is what eliminated the claims-desk
  finalist).
